**In the United States (as of mid-2026 data), Software Architects typically earn a base salary in the range of roughly $140,000–$170,000 on average, with total compensation often higher when including bonuses, equity, and other pay.**

Figures vary significantly by source, experience, location, company, and whether the number reflects base salary only or total pay.

### By Experience Level (Approximate US Base / Total)
- **Entry / Early career** (limited architecture experience): Often $100k–$130k.
- **Mid-level**: Commonly $135k–$170k base.
- **Senior / Staff**: $150k–$200k+ base; total compensation frequently $180k–$250k+.
- **Principal / Lead / Expert**: $200k–$300k+ total compensation is realistic at larger tech companies (especially with equity).

### Major Factors That Influence Pay
- **Location** — Highest in high-cost tech hubs (San Francisco Bay Area, New York, Seattle, Boston, Austin). Premiums of 20–30%+ vs. national average are common in SF/NY. Remote roles are often closer to national medians or slightly lower.
- **Company type** — Big Tech, high-growth startups, and finance often pay substantially more via stock/RSUs and bonuses (total comp can exceed $250k–$350k+ at top firms). Traditional enterprises or consulting may offer lower base but more stability.
- **Skills & specialization** — Cloud (AWS/Azure/GCP), distributed systems, security, AI/ML architecture, or domain expertise (e.g., fintech, healthcare) command premiums.
- **Industry** — Tech, finance, biotech/pharma, and telecommunications tend to pay more than average.
- **Additional compensation** — Bonuses, profit sharing, equity, and benefits can add 20–50%+ to base at many companies (especially visible in Glassdoor total-pay figures).

### International Snapshot
Salaries are generally lower outside the US (adjusted for cost of living and local markets). Examples include mid-level ranges often in the €70k–€120k+ area in Western Europe (higher in Switzerland or for principal roles at big tech), with freelancers sometimes billing €100–€150+/hour depending on the country.

**Salaries change with market conditions.** For the most personalized and current view, check sites like Levels.fyi (strong for tech/total comp), Glassdoor, Indeed, PayScale, or Robert Half’s salary guides, and filter by your city, years of experience, and target companies. Actual offers also depend heavily on negotiation, prior results, and the specific scope of the architect role (application vs. solution vs. enterprise).
